# Designing with AI Hero — Pass 4

Pass 4 preserves the approved Pass 3 scale, presentation primitives, content, and motion choreography.

It removes the technical background illustration, ambient field activity, and dark stage entirely. The former depth treatment now belongs to the panel: its white surface fades subtly toward gray at the far edge, while a light gray dimensional extrusion extends beyond the hero's unchanged layout height and dissolves into the white page. This intentional overflow creates more visual runway for the vertical entrance without changing document flow. The complete panel-and-extrusion assembly begins transparent at the bottom of that runway and eases to full opacity during the first half of its upward travel. The abstract workflow and interaction feedback remain intact.

Reduced-motion users continue to receive the complete final composition with the documented code/specification state visible and all looping motion removed.
